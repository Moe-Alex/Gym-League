\# GymLeague Privacy Policy



\*\*Effective date: 6 November 2026\*\*



GymLeague respects the privacy of its users and aims to collect only the information required to provide the features available in the application.



\## Information We Collect



When a user signs in to GymLeague using Google authentication, the application may collect basic account information such as the user's name, e-mail address and profile information provided through Google.



GymLeague also stores information created while using the application, including workout data, exercise activity, personal records, progress information, XP and level information, squad-related data and Battle Arena activity.



The application may also store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so that push notifications can be delivered to the user's device.



Some information may temporarily be stored locally on the user's device to support offline functionality and synchronisation.



\## How We Use Information



Information collected by GymLeague is used to:



\- authenticate users and maintain their account;

\- save and display workout and fitness progress;

\- provide squad and competitive features;

\- calculate and display progress, rankings and training information;

\- deliver relevant push notifications;

\- support offline functionality and synchronisation;

\- maintain application preferences and settings; and

\- improve the reliability and functionality of the application.



GymLeague does not use personal information for advertising purposes.



\## Where Information Is Stored



GymLeague uses a combination of cloud and local services.



Application data is processed through the GymLeague API hosted on Render and stored in the application's Neon database.



Firebase services are used for authentication and push notification functionality.



Some application data may also be stored locally on the Android device using Room Database to allow selected features to continue working while the device is offline.



\## Security



GymLeague communicates with its online services using HTTPS to protect information while it is being transmitted.



Authentication is handled using Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In.



Users may also choose to enable biometric unlock on supported Android devices. Biometric information such as fingerprints or facial data is handled by the Android operating system and is not stored by GymLeague.



\## Sharing of Information



GymLeague does not sell users' personal information.



Information is only processed through the services required for the application to operate, including Firebase, Render and Neon.



\## Data Retention and Deletion



User information is retained while it is required to provide GymLeague services.



A user may request deletion of their GymLeague account and associated personal information by contacting the GymLeague project team.



When a user logs out, locally stored session information is cleared from the device. Selected offline data may also be removed according to the application's offline storage behaviour.



\## Children's Privacy



GymLeague is a student-developed fitness application and is not specifically designed for children.



\## Changes to This Privacy Policy



This privacy policy may be updated if the application's features or data-handling practices change.



Any updated version will replace the previous version and include a revised effective date.



\## Contact



For privacy questions or requests relating to access or deletion of personal information, users can contact the GymLeague project team through the contact details provided with the application or project repository.
