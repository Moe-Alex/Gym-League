# App Title

GymLeague

# Short Description

Train smarter, track progress, compete with friends, and level up your fitness.

# Full Description

GymLeague is a fitness and competition app designed to make training more engaging, social and rewarding.

Track your workouts, monitor your progress, compete with other users and build consistency through a combination of training tools, challenges and performance tracking.

## Train and Track

Use the exercise library to explore exercises and log your workouts. GymLeague keeps track of your training history, personal records and progress so you can see how your performance develops over time.

## Battle Arena

Challenge other GymLeague users and compete through the Battle Arena. Track challenge progress, accept or decline challenges and see who comes out on top.

## Muscle Volume

Monitor training volume across different muscle groups and better understand how your workouts are distributed.

## Squads

Join and interact with squads to make training more social. GymLeague allows users to connect with others while working toward shared fitness goals.

## Progress and Rankings

Earn XP, increase your level and compare your progress through GymLeague rankings.

## Offline Support

GymLeague includes offline support using local device storage. Previously loaded information remains available when an internet connection is unavailable, and supported workout activity can be synchronised once the device reconnects.

## Push Notifications

Receive useful notifications for important GymLeague activity, including challenge and account updates.

## Multiple Languages

GymLeague supports English, Afrikaans and isiZulu, allowing users to choose their preferred language from Settings.

## Biometric Security

Users can optionally protect GymLeague with biometric authentication. After enabling biometric unlock, a supported fingerprint or face can be required before the application opens a logged-in session.

## Personalisation

Choose between light, dark or system appearance settings and customise notification and language preferences.

GymLeague combines workout tracking, competition, social features and progress monitoring into one fitness experience.

Train. Compete. Level up.
